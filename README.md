## About me

I hold an MD from Kermanshah University of Medical Sciences (2022)
and work as a clinical physician at Farabi Hospital's Neurology Center
in Kermanshah, Iran.

My research experience includes analyses of structural MRI,
brain volumetric measures, and cognitive assessments using data
from the Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative (ADNI).

I am interested in connecting clinical questions with computational
approaches to better understand neurological disorders.
I am currently developing practical skills in Python,
EEG analysis, neuroimaging, and machine learning.

## Research interests

- AI and machine learning for neurological disease research

## Research experience and ongoing work

- **ADNI-based research:** MRI volumetric and cognitive biomarker analyses
  relevant to Alzheimer's disease and mild cognitive impairment.
- **Evidence synthesis:** Co-author of an accepted systematic review
  and meta-analysis on automated structural MRI assessment of
  Alzheimer-related neurodegeneration.
- **Computational drug discovery:** An ongoing project investigating
  potential therapeutic targets in multiple sclerosis through
  molecular docking.
- **Preclinical neuroscience:** Collaborative work on hippocampal
  glial responses and neuroinflammation.
